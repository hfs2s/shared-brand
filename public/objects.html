How Shared works

The platform

A place to run

Shared sets up the 2lp runtime, nodes and seats an agent needs to do its work.

Lola runs on her own node and uses dedicated seats for Matt’s team. The setup follows the team’s workflow.

Collaboration

A place to meet

Teams work with agents in Juan Rooms, Juan Live and WhatsApp.

In Rooms, someone can ask Juan to show a slide while the team discusses it. The slide and the discussion belong in the same meeting.

Community agents

A memory for the group

Group permissions define who can ask an agent to do what. The group owns the memory of its work.

Milo runs the AI Engineers Barcelona podcast. A recurring show gives the community a continuing piece of work to share with its agent.

Learning to work together

Matt and Rafa lead advocacy and training. They help teams and communities learn to use their agents in everyday work, from making a request in a group chat to deciding who can ask for what.
